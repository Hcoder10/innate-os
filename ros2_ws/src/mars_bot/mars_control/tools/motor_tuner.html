Pitch

The whine sweeps between these within each gear; the ratio is the size of the sweep per gear.

Gearbox

Where each gear tops out, as a fraction of top speed. The whine snaps back at each shift and sweeps again — that gap is the strongest cue that the robot is accelerating.

Character

Damping under 1 makes the motor overshoot the robot instead of tracking it exactly. Brush flutter is the commutator buzz; accel growl is the rumble that comes in when it's working hard and fades at cruise.
