Drag the joystick, or use WASD. Sends drive commands to ws://localhost:8765, where drive_mars.py is listening. Space also works as an emergency stop. Respawn resets the robot to its start pose; Toggle Collision View swaps the textured render for the raw collision hulls (same as pressing "3" in the viewer).
